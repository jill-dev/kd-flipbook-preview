Sales Tax

Sales Tax will be added to all transactions. If you are tax exempt please fax your exemption certificate to (480) 455-3911 or email service@kdauctions.com before bidding. Utah requires sales tax unless the buyer provides a valid resale certificate or other accepted exemption (Utah accepts out-of-state resale certificates, including the MTC Uniform or SST Certificate). Utah's manufacturing exemption applies only to qualifying equipment used in a Utah manufacturing facility (Form TC-721). Items removed from Utah by a third-party common carrier are generally exempt, provided neither you nor your agent takes possession in Utah; items hauled on the buyer's own trucks are taxable. Exemption certificates must be submitted prior to invoicing and are subject to review and approval.
